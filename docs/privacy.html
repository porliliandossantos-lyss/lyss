Privacy Policy — Lyss

Last updated: 08/06/2026

Lyss is a personal, private-use content automation tool, operated by its developer solely to publish videos to her own TikTok account. It is not a product distributed or made available to the public.

What data Lyss accesses

When Lyss is authorized via TikTok login (OAuth), it receives an access token limited to the video.publish scope — used exclusively to publish videos to the authorizing account on behalf of its own owner. Lyss does not read, collect, or store data belonging to other TikTok users, comments, followers, or any information beyond what is needed to publish the generated content.

Where the data is stored

The access token is stored locally, on the machine where Lyss runs, and is never shared, sold, or transmitted to third parties. There is no public database or external service with access to that token besides TikTok's own official API.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time directly from TikTok account settings, under Settings and privacy → Security → Connected apps.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be opened as an issue in the project's repository: github.com/porliliandossantos-lyss/lyss/issues.
